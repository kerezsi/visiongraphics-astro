What is Aerial Integration?

We take real aerial photos and add your building to them. Simple as that. It shows exactly how your project will fit into the neighborhood when it’s done.

Hotel Adria Pearl (drone photo integration)

Why Use Aerial Integration?

Regular renderings can look great, but they don’t show the surroundings right. Aerial integrations fix that problem:

  • People see your building in its actual location
  • Shows the true scale compared to nearby buildings
  • Helps with planning approvals
  • Makes marketing more realistic
  • Lets you spot potential issues early

Planning boards love these because they can see exactly how your building relates to everything around it.

How We Create Them

We have a few ways to get the base photos:

  1. Airplane photography – we work with experienced pilots who can capture the perfect angle
  2. Helicopter shots – great for lower altitudes and precise positioning
  3. Drone photos – perfect for smaller sites or specific angles
  4. Google Earth – for initial studies in areas they cover in 3D

Once we have the base image, we:

  • Create a 3D model of your building
  • Match the exact perspective of the photo
  • Align the sunlight direction
  • Blend the shadows and reflections
  • Adjust the colors to match the photo’s conditions

The result looks natural because we’re careful about these details.

Bruesa Római (in aerial photos)

When to Use Aerial Integration

This works best when:

  • You need to show context and surroundings
  • Your project will change the neighborhood
  • You’re presenting to planning boards
  • Marketing to investors or buyers
  • Working on larger developments
