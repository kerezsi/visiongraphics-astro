What is Drone Photography?

It’s taking photos using unmanned flying cameras. Drones let us get shots from heights and angles that used to need helicopters or impossible camera rigging.

Swedsteel Metecno

Why Use Drones?

They get views you can’t get any other way:

  • Show buildings from just above rooftop level
  • Capture site context from mid-height
  • Get precise angles that tell your story
  • Access areas too difficult or dangerous for people
  • Create dynamic perspectives that impress viewers

Drone shots often become the hero images in marketing because they look so unique.

How We Capture Them

We either use our own equipment or work with specialist drone pilots:

  1. Plan the flight path and shot list
  2. Check weather conditions and time of day
  3. Handle permits and flight regulations
  4. Fly controlled paths for consistent results
  5. Process images for maximum impact

For sites with planned buildings, we do something special. We set up camera viewpoints using the 3D model first. Once you approve these angles, we match our 3D model to the real world and transfer exact coordinates to the drone. This way, we get perfect background photos that match the renderings we’ll create later – showing your building from only the best angles.

We can also capture 360° drone photos that let you look in all directions from a single point in the air. These work great for showing context or creating immersive views.

Safety and legality come first. We make sure all flights follow local regulations.

Novi Vinodolski, the plot of Hotel Adria Pearl

When to Use Drone Photography

This works perfectly when you need to:

  • Show buildings in their setting
  • Capture progress on construction
  • Document large properties
  • Get views from specific heights
  • See rooftops and upper floors
  • Create eye-catching marketing images
