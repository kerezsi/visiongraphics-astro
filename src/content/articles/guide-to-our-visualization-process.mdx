Every deliverable — stills, animations, 360° tours, VR, a web viewer — comes from the same foundation: a detailed 3D model. Understanding how that model gets built, and what branches from it, makes the whole process easier to navigate. One stage is new since we last wrote this guide: the AI finishing pass that now turns the working render into the delivered image.

<SectionBanner image="/_img/articles/process/brief.jpg" label="Stage 1" title="First conversation" />

We talk about what you need. Bring whatever you have — plans, sketches, a PDF, a rough brief. We help clarify the right outputs for your situation: what type of visualization, how many views, what timeline is realistic, what it will cost.

This is also when we catch scope issues early. A project that starts as "a few exterior renders" sometimes needs interior views, a 360 tour or a short film to actually do its job. Better to know at the start than three weeks in. Since 2026 there is a public [price list](/pricing/) and an [estimator](/pricing/estimate/) — most first conversations start from a package on that page and adjust it.

<SectionBanner image="/_img/articles/process/model.jpg" label="Stage 2" title="3D model" />

This is where the work begins. We build a detailed 3D model from your materials — structure, main architectural features, space planning. Everything that follows derives from this single source file.

What we need from you: floor plans, elevations, sections, material specifications if available, and reference images for the desired character. If you have 3D data (Revit, ArchiCAD, SketchUp, DWG) we work from that and the modelling cost drops accordingly — the price list halves the modelling line for a supplied 3D model and doubles it for a PDF or sketch.

<SectionBanner image="/_img/articles/archupgrade/atrium-before.jpg" label="Stage 3" title="Draft review" />

We produce initial views from the base model: basic materials, the main camera angles, rough lighting — enough to confirm layout, proportions and composition before we invest in detail.

![Draft-stage working render of an office atrium — correct geometry, camera and layout, nothing polished yet](/_img/articles/archupgrade/atrium-before.jpg)

This is the right moment to flag anything that looks wrong. A correction to the model at draft stage costs a fraction of what it costs after final materials, lighting and the finishing pass are in.

<SectionBanner image="/_img/portfolio/adria-pearl/01.jpg" label="Stage 4" title="Refinement and rendering" />

We update the model from your feedback, then build it out: architectural details, accurate materials, environmental context, precise lighting. This refined model becomes the master file for every output. V-Ray renders come out of it at the agreed resolution, and this is where the computation time goes — high-resolution stills and animation frames take real hours on the farm, which is why the timeline matters.

<SectionBanner image="/_img/articles/archupgrade/atrium-after.jpg" label="Stage 5" title="AI finishing and variants" />

This stage did not exist two years ago. The working render goes through our in-house AI pipeline, which produces photorealistic versions of every approved view — same geometry, same camera, same layout — in controlled variations: lighting mood, season, time of day, furnishing style. Each candidate is scored automatically on fidelity to the source, technical quality and style before a human looks at it. We pick from what passed; you can be part of that selection round if you want to be.

<ImageCompare before="/_img/articles/archupgrade/atrium-before.jpg" after="/_img/articles/archupgrade/atrium-after.jpg" beforeAlt="Working render of the office atrium" afterAlt="The delivered photorealistic version of the same view" beforeText="Working render" afterText="Delivered" label={false} />

What this changes for you: more options, earlier, and a shorter polishing stretch at the end of the schedule — the part that used to run late. A night or dusk variant of an approved view, or a different season, is a fraction of the cost of a new view. The details are in [From Render to Photograph](/articles/from-render-to-photograph-archupgrade/).

<SectionBanner image="/_img/portfolio/szombathely_lovas/01.jpg" label="Stage 6" title="Final output" />

With the refined model and the picked images, we produce the agreed deliverables at full quality.

**If stills are the final output:** upscaled to print resolution where needed, post-produced, delivered as high-resolution TIFF/JPG plus web-optimised versions.

**If animation is next:** camera paths, animated sequence, rendered frames, edit, music and narration — AI-composed music and AI narration are standard line items now, human voice and composition on request — delivered in your required format. For social and sales-office loops, [AI motion from stills](/vision-tech/ai-animation/) is the faster, cheaper route.

**If a 360° tour is next:** viewpoints are marked in the same 3D scene and the tour is generated from it — floor plan, links between rooms and all — so a re-render after a design change re-publishes the tour rather than rebuilding it. [How we built that](/articles/building-our-own-vr-tour-engine/).

**If interactive or VR is next:** we optimise the model for real time, build the Unreal Engine environment, configure the interactive features and package for web, desktop or headset.

## Final delivery

You receive everything in the formats your use case needs — print, web, presentation, or a hand-off package for your marketing team. Source files are included where contracted. Every delivered image carries a record of how it was made — including which AI tools touched it — so a version can be reproduced or branched later: "that one, but in evening light" is a re-run from the exact version, not a reconstruction from memory.

We archive project files for at least five years. The model can be updated for future phases or additional outputs without starting from scratch.

## Typical timelines

- Single exterior render: 3–5 days
- Multi-view package (5–10 stills): 1–2 weeks
- Interior package: 1–2 weeks
- Animation (1.5 min): 3–4 weeks
- VR / interactive: 4–6 weeks

The AI finishing pass shortens the last stretch of these, not the modelling. Rush timelines are possible with advance notice — and sometimes without it, depending on current load; the price list carries a rush multiplier so there is no surprise.

## What we need from you, in one list

1. Drawings — the more complete, the closer the first draft lands to the final result.
2. Material and finish intent, even rough.
3. A decision on what the images are *for* — planning, sales, an investor deck. It changes the camera, the hour of the day and the level of finish.
4. One person who can say yes.
