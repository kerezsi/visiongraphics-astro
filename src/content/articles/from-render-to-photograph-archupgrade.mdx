<Lang code="en">
Every visualization project hits the same wall. The 3D model is accurate, the cameras are approved, the layout is final, and then the polishing begins. Materials, lighting, vegetation, people, atmosphere. That last stretch from "correct render" to "image that sells the project" is where most of the time went.

Over the past year and a half I built a system, ArchUpgrade, that changes the economics of that stretch. It takes working 3D renders and turns them into photorealistic images, in controlled variations, with automated quality scoring on every output. Daily production use since spring 2026. It runs on my own hardware, on my own farm, and no client image leaves the building.

Drag the slider. One side is the working render out of 3ds Max, the other is the same image after the pipeline. Same geometry, same camera, same layout; the labels say which is which.

<Lang code="en">
These are look passes, not lighting simulations. The pipeline re-tints materials under the warm light it adds, so a daylight study still comes from the 3D model. For a brochure that needs the building at dusk, it is a fraction of the price of a night render, and the [price list](/pricing/) says so.

**Quality check before a human picks.** Each generated image is scored by a local AI reviewer on five aspects: aesthetics, fidelity to the source design, prompt adherence, technical quality, style match. Images below threshold are retried automatically. The review screen shows every candidate with its scores, so the final pick is made from a field that already passed inspection.

![The ArchUpgrade review screen — every generated image carries its quality scores](/_img/articles/archupgrade/archupgrade-review-grid.jpg)

**The design stays under control.** This is the part I care most about, and the part most "AI rendering" services get wrong. The pipeline works from the accurate 3D model. It enhances, it does not invent, unless I instruct it to. Walls do not move. Proportions do not drift. Brand colours stay brand colours. When a generated image deviates from the source design, the fidelity score catches it and the image is rejected.

## A project with this in the loop

1. **I build the 3D scene as always.** Accurate model from your plans, approved cameras, correct proportions. This part does not change. Everything else depends on it.
2. **The pipeline generates candidates.** For each approved view, a set of photorealistic versions across the moods and styles we agreed. A version renders in about a minute on one GPU; the farm runs many in parallel.
3. **Machines filter, humans choose.** Automated scoring removes the failures; I review what is left and pick. You can sit in on that round if you want to.
4. **Finishing on the picked images.** Relighting, sky, generative expand to a new aspect ratio, upscaling to print: passes on the chosen image rather than a return to the 3D scene.
5. **Delivery.** A clean, numbered package, with the full generation history archived on my side. If you come back in three months and want "that version, but in evening light", I branch from exactly that version.

## Beyond stills

The same system carries the rest of a presentation package: AI motion from stills for short films, AI narration and AI-composed music for them, upscalers for print, and experimental 3D tools, mesh generation for props, Gaussian splats for captured context. Those are line items on the price list where they are production-ready and internal experiments where they are not. I say which is which.

## What it is not

It does not replace 3D work. It is built on top of it. The accuracy comes from the model. The judgement about which image tells the right story is still mine, or yours. What the pipeline changes is the iteration count: more variations, tighter quality control, and a shorter polishing stretch at the end of every schedule.

## Egy projekt ezzel a hurokban

1. **A 3D-jelenetet úgy építem, mint mindig.** Pontos modell az Ön terveiből, jóváhagyott kamerák, helyes arányok. Ez a rész nem változik. Minden más ettől függ.
2. **A pipeline jelölteket generál.** Minden jóváhagyott nézethez fotorealisztikus változatok halmaza a megbeszélt hangulatokban és stílusokban. Egy változat egy GPU-n nagyjából egy perc alatt renderelődik; a farm sokat futtat párhuzamosan.
3. **A gépek szűrnek, az emberek választanak.** Az automatikus pontozás eltávolítja a selejtet; ami marad, azt átnézem és választok. Ha akar, beülhet abba a körbe.
4. **Befejezés a kiválasztott képeken.** Átvilágítás, égbolt, generatív bővítés új képarányra, felskálázás nyomdához: menetek a kiválasztott képen, nem visszatérés a 3D-jelenethez.
5. **Leszállítás.** Tiszta, számozott csomag, a teljes generálási előzmény az én oldalamon archiválva. Ha három hónap múlva visszajön, és azt kéri, „azt a változatot, de esti fényben", pontosan abból a változatból ágazok el.

## Mi nem

Nem helyettesíti a 3D-munkát. Ráépül. A pontosság a modellből jön. Az ítélet arról, hogy melyik kép meséli el a megfelelő történetet, továbbra is az enyém, vagy az Öné. Amit a pipeline megváltoztat, az az iterációk száma: több variáció, szorosabb minőségkontroll, és rövidebb csiszolási szakasz minden menetrend végén.
