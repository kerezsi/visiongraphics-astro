Every visualization brief hides the same small questions. Where does the sun actually stand over this plot in late October at four in the afternoon? What does a 24 mm lens do to the proportions of a lobby that a 50 mm keeps honest? Is this room warm because of the material palette, or because someone set the lights to 2,700 K? We answer these every week with a 3D model open and a deadline running. The tools that answer them are not complicated — they are just usually locked inside expensive software.

So we started taking them out. [Vision Graphics Labs](https://labs.visiongraphics.eu/) is a collection of free browser apps about light, colour, geometry and sound. Twenty-four are live; three more are on the way. They are made of the same skills we use to visualize buildings — small enough to give away.

## What's in the labs

The apps sort themselves into a few families.

**Light and shadow.** [Sunner](https://sunner.visiongraphics.eu) plots sun paths and shadows for any place on Earth — drag time around a wheel and watch shadows sweep across a model. [Warmmer](https://warmmer.visiongraphics.eu) re-lights one room from candlelight to overcast sky as you spin a Kelvin wheel. [Lensser](https://lensser.visiongraphics.eu) puts focal length, aperture, distance and sensor size on wheels and shows perspective and depth of field react live in 3D. [Shaddower](https://shaddower.visiongraphics.eu) turns the sun-path problem into a game: deduce the hour, the season and true north from shadows alone.

[![Sunner — the sun-path diagram for Budapest on a September noon, with the 3D massing box and its shadow on the right](/_img/articles/labs/sunner.jpg)](https://sunner.visiongraphics.eu)

**Colour.** [Paletter](https://paletter.visiongraphics.eu) builds OKLCH palettes from twelve harmony methods. [Mixxer](https://mixxer.visiongraphics.eu) asks you to mix one colour twice — with beams of light and with paint — and shows why the answers differ. [Differ](https://differ.visiongraphics.eu) makes you spot identical colours your eyes swear are different.

[![Paletter — a split-complementary palette on the OKLCH wheel, swatches ready to export](/_img/articles/labs/paletter.jpg)](https://paletter.visiongraphics.eu)

**Geometry and structure.** [Extrudder](https://extrudder.visiongraphics.eu) lets you trace a floor plan, pull it up into 3D and take the model home. [Vaultter](https://vaultter.visiongraphics.eu) covers 4,000 years from arch to vault — spin the timeline, drop the keystone, raise the vault in 3D. [Trusser](https://trusser.visiongraphics.eu) is a bridge-building game: survive the load with the least steel. [Cogger](https://cogger.visiongraphics.eu) sets gear-train puzzles; [Bounccer](https://bounccer.visiongraphics.eu) sets mirror, lens and prism puzzles with honest Snell's law.

[![Extrudder — tracing the walls of the demo floor plan before pulling them up into 3D](/_img/articles/labs/extrudder.jpg)](https://extrudder.visiongraphics.eu)

**Spatial thinking as a daily game.** [Viewwer](https://viewwer.visiongraphics.eu) is the classic spatial-aptitude drill — spin a 3D shape, spot its true flat view. [Sliccer](https://sliccer.visiongraphics.eu) asks for the cross-section before the blade lands. [Foldder](https://foldder.visiongraphics.eu) asks which net folds into which solid. [Rotatter](https://rotatter.visiongraphics.eu) asks same-or-mirrored against the clock. [Carvver](https://carvver.visiongraphics.eu) is boolean-operation golf: reach the target shape in the fewest carves. [Frammer](https://frammer.visiongraphics.eu) runs ten-second composition drills — crop, place the horizon, hit the thirds.

[![Viewwer — spin the 3D shape, then pick which of the four flat views is its true projection](/_img/articles/labs/viewwer.jpg)](https://viewwer.visiongraphics.eu)

**Sound and motion.** [Musiccer](https://musiccer.visiongraphics.eu) is a chord wheel with MIDI export; [Beatter](https://beatter.visiongraphics.eu) spins Euclidean rhythm circles with real drum synthesis. [Spiraller](https://spiraller.visiongraphics.eu) draws gear-driven rosettes you can export for a laser cutter; [Penduller](https://penduller.visiongraphics.eu) is a Victorian drawing machine of damped Lissajous figures; [Epiccer](https://epiccer.visiongraphics.eu) redraws any shape you sketch with spinning circles — Fourier series, made visible. [Planetter](https://planetter.visiongraphics.eu) puts the real solar system on two wheels and lets you dial the scale to honest.

[![Spiraller — a gear-drawn rosette mid-draw, ready for export to a laser cutter](/_img/articles/labs/spiraller.jpg)](https://spiraller.visiongraphics.eu)

Coming soon: Gearwalkker, Swingger and Ptolemmer — three more games about gear ratios, pendulums and epicycles.

## Why a studio gives these away

**They demonstrate the competence, not just describe it.** Anyone can write "we understand light" on a services page. Sunner shows a shadow sweeping a model as you drag the hour. Lensser shows what a wide lens does to a lobby. A client who has played with these for five minutes already knows what to ask us for.

**They are the same problems at a smaller scale.** A sun study for a planning submission, a lens choice for a marketing still, a palette for a brand rollout, a floor plan that needs to become a massing model by tomorrow — these are the daily questions of [architectural visualization](/services/architectural-visualization/). The labs are those questions with the project removed.

**They cost you nothing, in every sense.** Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Open one on a phone in a site meeting and it works.

## The honest position

These are toys. Sunner will not replace a proper solar study for a permit, and Extrudder will not replace the model we build from your CAD set. What they show is the kind of tool we build when a project needs one — the same competence that, at full scale, becomes a client-facing web viewer, a variant configurator or a launch microsite, all of which sit as fixed lines in our [price list](/pricing/#web). And if none of that is relevant to you, enjoy the toys.
